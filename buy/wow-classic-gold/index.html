Gold in World of Warcraft Classic

Gold is the lifeblood of Azeroth's economy in World of Warcraft Classic. From the first levels, it pays for training new spell ranks at your class trainer, reagents for spells and professions, and repairs after every dungeon run. As you progress, gold buys equipment from the Auction House, funds profession crafts such as blacksmithing, alchemy and enchanting, and covers the big milestone every Classic player remembers: the riding skill and your first mount. Raw materials — herbs, ore, leather, cloth and raid consumables — all move through the Auction House priced in gold, which makes it the universal measure of effort and progress. In Classic Era, where no tokens or modern shortcuts exist, a healthy gold balance is simply what keeps your character advancing.

Farming vs Buying

Farming gold yourself means long sessions of herb gathering, mining runs, dungeon farms or Auction House flipping, time that competes directly with raiding, PvP and levelling. Buying gold converts that grind into a few minutes of coordination: you keep your playtime for the content you enjoy and stop delaying consumables, mounts or gear upgrades.
