What is Adena?

Adena is the official currency of the world of Aden, the setting of Lineage 2. Named after the kingdom itself, it circulates through every town, castle and village in the game: merchants and grocers price their goods in adena, blacksmiths charge it for repairs and crafting, and warehouse fees, clan hall upkeep and castle taxes are all paid in the same coin. Players earn adena from monster drops, quests and trade, and spend it on weapons, armor, consumables such as soulshots and blessed spirits, item enhancements and player-to-player commerce in the town squares. Because almost every progression step consumes adena, a healthy balance is what keeps a character fighting, crafting and sieging without interruption.

Farming vs Buying

Farming adena yourself means long hunting sessions, repeated drop luck and time spent traveling, warehousing and selling loot to other players. It is reliable but slow, and it competes with the time you could spend on quests, clans or sieges. Buying adena converts that grind into a few minutes of coordination: you keep your playtime for the content you actually enjoy while your currency balance stops being the bottleneck.
