What is Yang in Metin2?

Yang is the universal currency of Metin2, shared by the three rival kingdoms of the game world: Shinsoo, Chunjo and Jinno. Whatever side you chose at character creation, every merchant, blacksmith and shopkeeper prices their goods in yang. It pays for potions and arrows, equipment upgrades at the blacksmith, socketing and refining with stones, and the exchange of items between players through the trade window or the local markets. Boss drops, metin stones and regular monster farm all convert into yang sooner or later, which makes it the true measure of a character's progress. Because upgrade attempts, epic item crafts and rare stones consume large sums, most players eventually face a yang shortage exactly when they want to push their gear forward.
